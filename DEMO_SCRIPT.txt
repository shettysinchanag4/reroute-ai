[0:20 — WHAT IT IS]
Welcome to RerouteAI. RerouteAI is an autonomous, multi-agent travel disruption recovery concierge built using Google ADK 2.11 and Gemini 2.5 Flash. It belongs to the Concierge Agents track. Instead of only notifying you about a broken flight, RerouteAI analyzes your entire multi-modal journey, cascades the impact downstream across all dependent reservations, synthesizes four distinct recovery plans, and securely rebooks your travel with your explicit approval.

[1:00 — ARCHITECTURE]
(show assets/architecture_diagram.png)

Let us take a look under the hood at our ADK 2.11 Workflow Graph.
When a disruption event arrives, it first enters our load_input node.
From there, it routes to our security_checkpoint node. This node scrubs sensitive PII like Aadhaar, PAN cards, and phone numbers, and evaluates the text against prompt injection heuristics. If an attack is detected, it immediately branches to security_blocked.
When the payload is clean, it triggers the RerouteOrchestrator. The orchestrator delegates to three specialist LlmAgents using ADK's AgentTool:
First, DisruptionDetectorAgent queries our MCP Server via stdio to fetch root booking details.
Second, ImpactAnalyzerAgent retrieves the full itinerary and computes which downstream legs are broken.
Third, RecoveryPlannerAgent searches alternative flights, trains, and cabs through the MCP inventory to formulate four ranked plans.
Finally, the workflow transitions to human_approval, which triggers an ADK RequestInput pause so the traveler can review and approve before process_approval confirms the rebooking.
